EML Viewer and MSG File Viewer

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

EML Viewer and MSG File Viewer is a Chrome extension that opens .eml and .msg email files. It works entirely in your browser. We don’t collect, store or sell any personal data.

Your email files

  • Files you open are read and shown on your computer. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • The extension has no servers, no accounts and no sign-in.
  • If an email contains pictures stored online, your browser may load them from where the sender put them, just like a mail app does.

Recent emails and settings

  • The extension keeps a list of recently opened emails (up to 50, for up to 90 days) so you can open them again. It stays in your browser on this computer only.
  • You can turn this list off or clear it at any time in the extension’s settings. Removing the extension deletes it too.
  • Your settings, such as the theme, are also stored only in your browser.

Gmail and Outlook

  • Access to Gmail or Outlook is off until you turn it on in the extension. You can turn it off at any time.
  • When it’s on, the extension adds an open button to .eml and .msg attachments on those sites. It reads only the attachment you click, and only to show it to you.
  • The extension doesn’t read, send or change your other messages.

This website

  • emlview.com has no analytics, no cookies and no ads.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to keep its service secure. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date above.
